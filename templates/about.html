About this archive

NDA Explorer is a plain-English window onto Hungary's National Digital Archive (NDA) - a catalogue of digitised cultural works held by SZTAKI, published as Linked Open Data. Every answer here is generated by translating your question into a query against that catalogue, so it only ever tells you what's actually recorded in it.

What's in the catalogue

Each work carries a mix of the following, where recorded:

  • A title, description and publisher
  • A creator, linked by name
  • A content type - sound, text, image or moving image
  • A subject or keyword, a date, and a language
  • The series it belongs to, if any

How a question gets answered

When you ask something, it's first checked against what this catalogue actually tracks. If it's in scope, it's turned into a query, run against the archive, and the results are summarised in plain language - with a chart drawn automatically when the data suits one. Nothing is answered from general knowledge; if the catalogue has no matching record, you're told that directly rather than given a guess.
